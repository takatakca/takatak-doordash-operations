# Evidence and reconciliation checks

Keep completed registers and all source evidence in authorized private storage. This public repository contains empty schemas only.

## Case coverage

Inspect each accessible business separately. A group-level case list may not include every business. Record date range, status filters, unread threads and lookup errors. No results within a filter is not proof that no older cases exist. Read original threads before replying; automated page summaries are not evidence of resolution.

## Charge review

Distinguish error charges, card chargebacks, tablet rental, marketing and unexplained adjustments. Record individual charge lines and credits. Repeated amounts for one order require source verification; they are not automatically duplicate charges. Separate historical claimed balances from independently verified current balances and confirmed refunds. Never add a subtotal again when it is already included in a larger category.

If the per-order deadline has passed, record that limitation and ask the existing billing owner whether manual review is available. Do not assume an appeal is eligible. Obtain an itemized written decision and credit references. Reconcile credits issued after the original request before claiming a remaining balance. Do not accept a settlement or paid service without specific user direction.

## Routing

Record protocol and activation as separate fields. Historical support confirmation of a tablet protocol does not prove current activation, provisioning or successful order receipt. A closed integration case does not establish operational readiness. Verify brand, premises and menu mapping before any migration or consolidation.

## Exports

Record report generation, availability, download, private archival and reconciliation as separate stages. A generated report with a Download button is not an archived original. Verify actual store coverage. Keep complete calendar months separate from current month-to-date, record the last included full day, and do not call a custom financial export an official monthly statement unless its source establishes that.

## Handoff

Preserve source threads and exact blockers privately. Keep a dated snapshot with its generation date and explain that it is not ongoing synchronization. Never copy populated registers, correspondence, financial values or private links into this public repository.
